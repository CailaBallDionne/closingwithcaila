Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026. The short version: I collect what you choose to send me plus basic visit data, I use it to respond to you and improve this site, and I do not sell it.

Who this covers

This policy covers closingwithcaila.com, which is run by Caila Ball-Dionne, a licensed California real estate agent (CA DRE #02439602) with The Borges Real Estate Team at eXp Realty. Questions about it are welcome at caila@theborgesrealestateteam.com or 626-657-7972.

What I collect

Information you give me:

  • Contact form: your name, email, phone number (optional), whether you are looking to buy, sell, both, or just explore, and any message you write.
  • Foothills Reads: the book, the library you pick or suggest (including a nickname or cross streets), your review, and a name or initials if you want one. If you check a box asking me to follow up, I also collect the email or phone number you enter.

Information collected automatically:

  • Google Analytics: pages you view, how long you stay, how you got here, your approximate location (such as city), and your device and browser type. Google Analytics uses cookies or similar technologies to do this.
  • Hosting logs: my hosting provider, Netlify, may keep standard technical logs, such as IP address and browser type.
  • Files from other providers: when you load this site, your browser also connects to services that supply fonts, code libraries, and map tiles (Google Fonts, unpkg, and OpenStreetMap on the Foothills Reads page). Those providers can see your IP address and browser information.

What is public

On Foothills Reads, the book title, library, review, and the name or initials you enter appear on the public map and feed. I do not display your email or phone number on the site. Please keep personal contact details out of the review itself.

How I use it

  • To reply to your message and follow up if you ask me to
  • To run Foothills Reads, including adding library locations to the map
  • To understand how people use this site so I can improve it

Who I share it with

  • Service providers that help run the site: Formspree (delivers contact form messages to my email), Google (Analytics, plus Google Sheets and Apps Script for Foothills Reads), and Netlify (hosting).
  • My brokerage: if you tell me you are looking to buy or sell, I may share your information with eXp Realty as needed to work with you.
  • When the law requires it.

I do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and Do Not Track

Google Analytics uses cookies to recognize repeat visits. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's opt-out browser add-on to stop Analytics from collecting your visit data. Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" setting that asks websites not to track you. There is no common standard for how sites should respond to it, and this site does not change how it works when the setting is on. The cookie and opt-out options above are the way to limit tracking here.

Your choices

You can ask me what information I have from you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it, including a Foothills Reads review. Email me and I will respond as soon as I can.

Links to other sites

This site links to other sites, including Instagram, my brokerage's website, and the home search at caila.lametrohomefinder.com. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and this policy does not cover them.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

I take reasonable steps to protect the information you send me, but no website or email is completely secure. Please do not send sensitive details, like a Social Security number or bank account information, through the forms on this site.

Changes to this policy

If I update this policy, I will post the new version here with a new effective date.
